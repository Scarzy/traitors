Rules

  • • You must never reveal your identity or give someone your phone
  • • If you delete cookies you will permanently lose access to your user and have to start again
  • • A round lasts 1 hour.
  • • During each hour you should vote to banish a player.
  • • During each hour traitors should vote to murder or recruit a player.
  • • The aim is to finish with the most points.
  • • Points can be earned during the activities. Different activities earn different amounts of points, and it may be dependent on how you do in the activity.
  • • Each hour there will different activities to take part in. You can choose whether to take part or not.
  • • Shields exist that can protect you from murders for 2 hours.
  • • Shields can be earned either by taking part in certain activities or by buying them.
  • • Points can be traded for different perks, e.g. a song choice on box, voting for different activities later on, or to buy a shield. These can be bought in the perks page of the app.
  • • 2 traitors have to vote to murder the same person for them to be murdered. If only 1 traitor votes then the target becomes wounded.
  • • The top 3 people voted to be banished each hour will be banished.
  • • Murdered and banished players will lose 250 points.
  • • Murdered and banished players will be unable to vote to banish.
  • • Murdered, wounded, and banished players will only earn half points in the activities.
  • • Murders and banishments last for 3 hours.
  • • Wounded players will only be wounded for 1 hour.
  • • A banished Traitor will re-enter as a Faithful, and will not be eligible for recruitment.
  • • A murdered or banished Faithful will re-enter as a Faithful and will be eligible for recruitment.
  • • Any Traitors still standing
  • • Traitors will earn points every time they murder/recruit.
  • • Points will only update at the time of a murder/banishment.
